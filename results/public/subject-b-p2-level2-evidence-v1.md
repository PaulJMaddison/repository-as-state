# Subject-B P2 Level-2 evidence

## Scope and design

P2 is an accepted Level-2 same-repository replication: four tasks, three repetitions, two conditions, 24 executions, 12 matched task-by-repetition pairs, and 13 governing behaviours across the four task contracts. The corrected denominator is therefore 39 behaviour observations per condition.

Condition A was persistent-session and Condition B was fresh-session.

## Execution and adjudication integrity

All 24 frozen Item-62 candidates were retained without rerun or modification. The accepted Item-63 recovery performed 24 blinded semantic adjudications, used no condition information during Stage-A scoring, and froze all 24 records before the single permitted unblinding.

The first Item-63 attempt remains preserved as invalid history: it made 24 placeholder verifier calls, produced zero valid adjudications, unblinded prematurely, and then made one sealed-verifier diagnostic execution whose result was excluded. The accepted recovery used 24 fresh real-verifier adjudications. A later denominator arithmetic defect was repaired only in derived reporting; the frozen Stage-A evidence was unchanged.

## Accepted P2 result

No behavioural correctness difference was observed between the persistent-session and fresh-session conditions in P2: both conditions satisfied 0 of 39 evaluated behaviour observations, with 39 matched agreements and no disagreements.

Both conditions failed all 39 evaluated behaviours, and candidate-level overall verifier passes were 0/12 in each condition. There were no disagreements favouring either condition.

## Floor effect and interpretation

Because both conditions were at the floor of the P2 correctness measure, this P2 result does not demonstrate successful behavioural preservation by either condition and cannot by itself be interpreted as evidence of behavioural equivalence or repository-state sufficiency.

P1 remains contextual and is not pooled with P2: P1 observed equal non-zero performance, 18/30 versus 18/30, with 30 agreements and no disagreements. P2 observed equal zero performance and therefore provides no positive replication of P1's successful behavioural-preservation observation.

Resource and reconstruction telemetry remain separate descriptive evidence. No cost, speed, or resource-superiority claim is made. The result is limited to this subject, repository, model/runtime family, task sample, and protocol. Subject C and further independent cross-repository replication remain required.
