## Most dangerous reviewer attacks

1. Handoff Debt already shows repository-only resumption can be expensive.
2. The original A/B conditions were not causally isolated because a session reset can also reset tools/workspace/caches/provider state.
3. Git-bound repository memory already exists, so the broad novelty claim is gone.
4. A private author-designed repository can strongly favour the hypothesis.
5. A five-task pilot cannot establish non-inferiority or general economic advantage.

If the paper cannot answer those five directly, it is not ready even for P0.
