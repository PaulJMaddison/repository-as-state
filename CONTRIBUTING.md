# Contributing

Repository-as-State is being developed as a reproducible software-engineering / AI-systems research project.

Contributions should be small enough to review, explicit about the claim category they affect, and accompanied by tests or validation where behaviour changes. Empirical contributions must identify the protocol version, condition, task identifier, model/runtime configuration, repository revision, and sanitisation status.

Do not submit proprietary source, private prompts containing proprietary source, credentials, customer data, or unverifiable empirical claims.

For manuscript changes, prefer primary sources and add complete bibliography metadata. If a claim is substantive, update paper/claims-register.md when necessary.

By contributing research software, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under Apache-2.0. Contributions to manuscript text remain subject to applicable copyright and publication terms.
