# P0 interpretation rules

P0 is a methodology pilot / initial private case study. It is not powered to establish population-level superiority or non-inferiority.

## Allowed top-level classifications

- **METHODOLOGY_FAILURE**
- **EVIDENCE_AGAINST_RAS**
- **MIXED_OR_CONDITIONAL_EVIDENCE**
- **SUPPORTIVE_PILOT_EVIDENCE**

Never classify P0 as:

- PROVEN;
- VALIDATED_GENERALLY;
- SUPERIOR.

## Two independent dimensions

### A. Continuity

**supportive** — forced reset completes the matched dependent sequence comparably to persistent history in this pilot without a material task-level collapse.

**mixed** — reset succeeds for some boundaries/tasks but loses continuity at others, or interpretation is conditional on task depth/type.

**adverse** — persistent history succeeds where forced reset materially fails, the reset chain collapses, or accepted state is repeatedly insufficient.

### B. Economic/reconstruction proxy

**favourable** — observable reconstruction/total resource burden is lower enough to motivate further testing, with comparable pilot outcomes.

**mixed** — some resources improve while others worsen or reconstruction burden is substantial.

**adverse** — rediscovery dominates or total outcome-normalised observable resource use is no better/worse.

**unobservable** — telemetry is too incomplete for a defensible proxy judgement.

A legitimate result is:

> continuity supportive; reconstruction economics adverse.

## Methodology override

If treatment isolation, verifier validity, future-history isolation, task specification, or other mandatory causal machinery fails, classify **METHODOLOGY_FAILURE** rather than evidence for or against RaS.

## Falsification remains possible

Evidence against RaS includes:

- B fails tasks A passes;
- B collapses with reset depth;
- reconstruction dominates time/context;
- fresh reasoners repeatedly misunderstand prior accepted architecture;
- accepted repository state is insufficient;
- required semantic state becomes excessive or unusually tailored.
